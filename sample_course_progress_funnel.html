The halfway effect

Likelihood of finishing, by furthest point reached

Reached…Went on to complete

Key finding: once a user starts a course, the single largest drop-off is the 30% → 40% transition (−29%). But users who push past it and reach the halfway point complete at ~85%. Halfway is the retention inflection: cross it and the finish line is nearly assured.
